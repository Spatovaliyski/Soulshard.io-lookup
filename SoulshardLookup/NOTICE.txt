Soulshard Lookup uses native World of Warcraft fonts, icons and talent paintings. World of Warcraft artwork belongs to Blizzard Entertainment.

Raid journal pictures are Blizzard Entertainment's World of Warcraft Dungeon Journal artwork, obtained from https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/journal/ui-ej-dungeonbutton-{name}.png. The offline TGA images are cropped versions of that artwork.

Alliance and Horde crests are Blizzard Entertainment's World of Warcraft faction emblems.

Talent tree reference data and spell icon names adapted from WoWSims. Six additional TBC icon names were verified against https://nether.wowhead.com/tbc/tooltip/spell/{id} (33891, 31846, 41021, 33174, 31233, 30669). Live character ranks remain sourced from Blizzard. World of Warcraft artwork belongs to Blizzard Entertainment.
